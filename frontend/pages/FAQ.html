Frequently Asked Questions

How do I rent a vehicle?

expand_more

To rent a vehicle, simply search for your desired location and dates on our platform. Browse available vehicles, select the one that fits your needs, and follow the prompts to complete your booking and payment.

Can I cancel a booking?

expand_more

Yes, you can cancel a booking. Our cancellation policies vary depending on the host and the timing of the cancellation. Please review the specific cancellation policy for your booking for detailed information on refunds and potential fees.

Is there a service fee?

expand_more

Yes, Rentify charges a service fee to both renters and hosts to help operate our platform and provide services like customer support and insurance options. This fee is calculated from the booking subtotal and is shown to you before you confirm your reservation.

What payment methods are accepted?

expand_more

We accept all major credit and debit cards, including Visa, MasterCard, and American Express. All payments are processed securely through our payment provider.

How does verification work?

expand_more

For the safety of our community, we require users to verify their identity. This typically involves uploading a government-issued ID and a selfie. The process is quick, secure, and is usually completed within minutes.

What happens if I have an issue during my rental?

expand_more

If you encounter any issues during your rental period, such as a vehicle breakdown or an accident, please contact your host immediately. For emergencies, dial your local emergency number first. You can also contact Rentify support 24/7 for assistance.
