Verification Help

Verification is a crucial step to ensure the safety and trust of our community. It helps us confirm your identity, prevent fraud, and enables you to receive payments and publish your vehicle listings.

Our team typically reviews submitted documents within 1-3 business days. You will be notified via email as soon as the review is complete.

We accept valid, government-issued photo IDs such as a driver's license, passport, or national ID card. For proof of address, you can use a recent utility bill or bank statement.

Common rejection reasons include blurry or unreadable documents, expired IDs, mismatched names between documents and your profile, or a selfie that is not clear. Please ensure all submitted documents are clear and current.

Account Issues

You can change your display name in the 'Settings' tab. Navigate to the 'Profile' section and click 'Edit' next to your name. Please note that for security reasons, name changes may require re-verification.

If you've forgotten your password, click the 'Forgot Password' link on the login page. You'll receive an email with instructions on how to reset it. If you're still having trouble, please contact our support team.

Your contact information, including email and phone number, can be updated in the 'Settings' tab under the 'Account' section. Keeping your information current is important for account security and communication.

To delete your account, please go to the 'Settings' tab and find the 'Account Deletion' section. Please be aware that this action is permanent and will remove all your data, including listings and payment history.

Listing Problems

If your vehicle isn't showing up, first check your 'My Listings' page. If the status is 'Draft', it means your account needs to be verified before the listing can be published. If your account is verified and the issue persists, please contact support.

You can edit your vehicle's details by navigating to the 'My Listings' tab. Find the vehicle you wish to update and click the 'Edit' button. You can then modify information like price, description, and photos.

If you're having trouble uploading photos, ensure they meet our requirements: PNG, JPG, or GIF format, and under 10MB in size. A stable internet connection is also recommended. If problems continue, try clearing your browser's cache or using a different browser.

If a listing is rejected, we will provide a reason via email and in your dashboard notifications. Common reasons include inaccurate information or photos that don't meet our quality standards. You can correct the issues and resubmit the listing for review.

General FAQ

Flexify is a peer-to-peer vehicle sharing marketplace. We connect vehicle owners with individuals who need to rent a car, providing a safe, secure, and convenient platform for both parties to transact.

Renters can browse available vehicles, select dates, and send a booking request. As an owner, you will receive a notification and can choose to accept or decline the request. Once accepted, the booking is confirmed, and you coordinate the handover.

Flexify charges a service fee to both the owner and the renter for each completed trip. This fee covers platform costs, insurance, and 24/7 support. The exact percentage is detailed in your 'Payments' tab and during the listing process.

You can contact our support team directly through the contact options listed on this page, including email, WhatsApp, or by creating a support ticket. We're here to help with any questions or issues you may have.
